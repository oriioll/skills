# Legal page drafts (used by `/audit fix legal`)

> Drafts, **not legal advice**. Every generated page starts with a visible-in-source comment:
> `<!-- DRAFT generated by /audit. Fill every [PLACEHOLDER] and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before publishing. -->`

## Rules for generating
1. **Match the code**: include only the purposes, processors, cookies and data flows found in the inventory (`legal-eu-es.md`). Never claim practices the code does not implement (e.g. "we do not use cookies" while analytics loads).
2. **Never invent business facts.** Use placeholders and list them all in the final report:
   `[LEGAL_NAME]` `[TAX_ID]` `[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]` `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` `[PHONE_OPTIONAL]` `[REGISTRY_DATA]` `[DPO_CONTACT_IF_ANY]` `[HOSTING_PROVIDER_AND_LOCATION]` `[RETENTION_PERIODS]` `[SITE_URL]` `[LAST_UPDATED_DATE]`
3. **Language**: write in the site's language(s). For a Spanish audience, Spanish is required; use standard terms: *responsable del tratamiento, base jurídica, encargado del tratamiento, finalidad, plazo de conservación, derechos ARSULIPO (acceso, rectificación, supresión, limitación, portabilidad, oposición)*, *Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)*.
4. **Place them in the project's own structure and style**, reusing its layout and components: Vue `src/views/*.vue` + routes; Angular standalone components + routes; Next `app/<slug>/page.tsx`; vanilla `<slug>.html`. Slugs: `/privacy` `/cookies` `/terms` `/legal-notice` (Spanish site: `/privacidad` `/cookies` `/terminos` `/aviso-legal`). Add footer links, sitemap entries, and a "Cookie settings" link.
5. Show the user the drafts and the placeholder list, and **ask once for confirmation** before writing files (see SKILL.md section 6).

## C. Terms and conditions (*Términos y condiciones / Condiciones de uso*)

1. Owner identification (link to legal notice).
2. Purpose and scope of the site/service; eligibility (age).
3. Accounts and security (if accounts exist).
4. Acceptable use and prohibited conduct.
5. Intellectual and industrial property; user content licence (if user content exists).
6. **If ecommerce/services to consumers**: prices and taxes included, payment methods, delivery, **right of withdrawal 14 days** (exceptions, model form), returns, legal warranty, complaints channel, order confirmation.
7. Liability limits (without excluding rights that cannot be waived by consumers), force majeure.
8. Modifications, termination, severability.
9. Governing law (Spain) and jurisdiction (consumers keep their own courts), contact, last update.

## E. Cookie banner copy (first layer)

- Title: "We use cookies" (*Usamos cookies*)
- Text: 1-2 sentences: necessary cookies always on; with your consent we also use [analytics/marketing] to [purpose]. Link to the cookie policy.
- Buttons, **equal visual weight**: "Accept all" · "Reject all" · "Configure"
- Second layer: toggles per category (necessary locked on, others off by default), list of providers, "Save preferences".
- Footer link on every page: "Cookie settings" reopening the preferences.

Implementation notes: initialise the consent library at app start (Vue `main.ts`, Angular `main.ts`/root component, Next client component in `layout`, vanilla `<script>`); gate scripts by category (`type="text/plain" data-category="analytics"` with `vanilla-cookieconsent`, or dynamic loading after consent callback); keep the consent record versioned.
